A leaf is a green above-ground plant organ. Its main functions are photosynthesis and gas exchange. They regulate carbon dioxide, oxygen, and water vapour exchange with the atmosphere. Before this happens, the leaves change colour. Leaves are normally green in color, which comes from chlorophyll found in the chloroplasts.
